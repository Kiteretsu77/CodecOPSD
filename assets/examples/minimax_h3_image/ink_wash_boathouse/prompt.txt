The East Asian ink-wash painting in <Picture 1> is <Subject 1>; its style is bold black brush strokes, soft grey washes and large areas of empty pale rice paper. The red boathouse on the misty alpine lake in <Picture 2> is <Subject 2>. The target image is a new ink-wash painting in exactly the style of <Subject 1>, composed as a tall, narrow vertical hanging scroll, showing the scene of <Subject 2>: at the bottom the boathouse on stilts with its rowing boat on the still lake and above it the pine shore and high misty mountains rising to the top of the scroll, painted in black ink and grey washes with only the boathouse in a touch of vermilion red. The style of <Subject 1> is fully preserved. No text, no signature and no seal.
